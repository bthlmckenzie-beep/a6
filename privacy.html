Privacy Standards & Confidentiality

Last Revised: March 2026

1. Client Data Protection Commitment

At CoutureFolio, client confidentiality is as sacred as our textile savoir-faire. We respect the personal privacy of our patrons, research subscribers, and atelier visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect, how it is processed, and our strict non-disclosure commitments.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when submitting a private consultation request, subscribing to our editorial journal, or corresponding with our atelier. This includes your name, email address, telephone contact, and bespoke garment preferences.

3. Zero-Sale & Non-Disclosure Commitment

CoutureFolio maintains a strict policy: we never sell, rent, monetize, or trade client personal information or anatomical measurement records with third-party advertising networks or commercial brokers. All bespoke client measurement profiles are encrypted and stored in secure archival vaults.

4. Analytical Cookies & Preferences

We employ minimal session cookies and aggregated analytics (via Google Analytics G-0LY0HY7L01) strictly to monitor website performance, understand editorial readership, and preserve your chosen display mode (Atelier Noir or Silk Studio). You may disable cookies through your browser settings without impacting website accessibility.

5. Contacting Our Data Steward

For privacy inquiries or to request permanent erasure of your consultation records, please contact our administrative bureau at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or email dispatch at privacy@couturefolio.com.
