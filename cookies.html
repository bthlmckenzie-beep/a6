Cookie Policy & Preferences

Last Revised: March 2026

1. Purpose of Cookies

Cookies are small text data fragments stored on your device when you browse websites. CoutureFolio uses cookies solely to improve your reading experience, remember your aesthetic display preference (Atelier Noir or Silk Studio Light), and analyze aggregated website traffic patterns.

2. Cookies Deployed on CoutureFolio

Cookie Type Provider & Identifier Purpose & Retention
Theme Preference Local Storage (couture_theme) Stores your selection between Atelier Noir and Silk Studio light mode. Persistent until cleared.
Web Analytics Google Analytics (G-0LY0HY7L01) Aggregated, anonymized statistics on page readership, session durations, and bounce rates.
Session Security CoutureFolio Core Engine Maintains basic cryptographic form token validation for consultation submissions.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You may modify, block, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not restrict your ability to read our technical publications or submit consultation inquiries.
